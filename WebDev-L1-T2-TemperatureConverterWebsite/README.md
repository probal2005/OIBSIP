## 🔢 Conversion Formulas

### Celsius → Fahrenheit

```text
°F = (°C × 9/5) + 32


### Celsius → Kelvin

```text
K = °C + 273.15
```

### Fahrenheit → Celsius

```text
°C = (°F − 32) × 5/9
```

### Fahrenheit → Kelvin

```text
K = (°F − 32) × 5/9 + 273.15
```

### Kelvin → Celsius

```text
°C = K − 273.15
```

### Kelvin → Fahrenheit

```text
°F = (K − 273.15) × 9/5 + 32
```
